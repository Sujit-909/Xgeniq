# **App Name**: XgenIq Digital Hub

## Core Features:

- Homepage Showcase: Displays the company's brand, taglines, hero section, and a compelling overview of their value proposition.
- About XgenIq: Presents detailed information about the company's vision, leadership (Dbasmita Behera, Debananda Behera), and commitment to digital transformation.
- Comprehensive Services Portfolio: Details all core service offerings including Custom Software Development, AI & Machine Learning Solutions, SAP Consulting, and Bid & Reverse Auction Systems.
- Unique Selling Proposition Highlights: Showcases XgenIq's key differentiators such as AI-driven solutions, scalable architecture, domain expertise, and customized client approach.
- Client Inquiry Form: A user-friendly form for prospective clients to submit their project requirements, describe business challenges, and request consultations or quotes.
- AI Service Matchmaker Tool: An interactive tool that leverages generative AI to analyze user inputs from the inquiry form, suggesting the most relevant XgenIq service categories or a preliminary solution outline tailored to their business challenges.
- Footer Navigation & Contact Info: Provides essential website navigation links, copyright information, and primary contact details like email (info@xgeniq.com) and phone.

## Style Guidelines:

- A sophisticated, dark color scheme reflecting a modern and intelligent tech brand. The primary interactive color will be a deep, vibrant purple-blue (#682BF8), symbolizing innovation and digital depth. The background will be a heavily desaturated variant of this hue, appearing as a very dark slate (#1C1A1F), to provide a premium feel and high contrast. A luminous accent blue (#93A5FF) will be used for calls to action and highlights, ensuring visual prominence and energy.
- Headlines will utilize 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif), lending a contemporary, tech-inspired feel. Body text will be set in 'Inter' (sans-serif) for its excellent readability and neutral, objective appearance, ideal for professional content.
- Geometric, abstract icons will be used throughout, subtly referencing concepts like data flow, artificial intelligence, scalability, and secure connections, maintaining a clean and futuristic aesthetic.
- A clean, modern layout characterized by spacious sections, clear content hierarchy, and strong alignment. The design will be responsive, ensuring a consistent premium experience across all device sizes.
- Subtle, sleek micro-animations on interactive elements and section transitions will enhance user engagement without distracting from the professional content. Animations will prioritize performance and a fluid digital experience, reinforcing the company's focus on next-generation technology.
